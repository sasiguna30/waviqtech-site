# Homepage service icons

Generic teal SVGs (sms, contacts, funnel, chart, browser, cart, gears, pen, article) are original artwork created for this homepage. No third-party icon library is used.

Brand SVGs were downloaded from Meta’s official Brand Resource Center on 2026-09-20 and copied without modifications (only local filenames changed):

- `whatsapp.svg`: [WhatsApp brand assets and guidelines](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/), download “WhatsApp-Brand-Resource-Center.zip”; original member `Brand Resource Center Page Downloads/01_Glyph/01_Digital RGB/03_SVG/Digital_Glyph_Green_RGB_2026.svg`.
- `messenger.svg`: [Messenger icon](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/messenger-icon/), download “Messenger-Icon-Pack.zip”; original member `Messenger Icon/Primary Icon/Messenger_Icon_Primary_Blue.svg` (February 2025 icon).

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners and are subject to the linked brand guidelines, rather than an open-source license. Preserve their original colors and proportions. All icons are served locally, rendered at 32px, and hidden from assistive technology because adjacent headings supply their meaning.
