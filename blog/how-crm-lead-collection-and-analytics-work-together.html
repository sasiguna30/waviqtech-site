How CRM, Lead Collection and Analytics Work Together

Follow an enquiry from collection to team follow-up and reporting, with clearer ownership at each step.

By Waviq Technologies · Introductory guide

Collect information with a purpose

A useful enquiry form asks for enough information to take the next step. That might include a business contact, the requested service and a preferred way to reply. Avoid collecting extra details merely because a form allows it. Explain how the information will be used and obtain appropriate consent where required.

Give each enquiry a home in the CRM

The CRM can store the contact, enquiry source, relevant permissions and current status. Define what each status means: a new enquiry, a request awaiting details or a quotation under review. Assign an owner so that a record does not sit in a shared list without anyone responsible for it. Decide how duplicate enquiries will be recognized before connecting multiple sources.

Connect actions to reporting

A dashboard can summarize enquiry volumes, follow-up activity and the number of records in each stage. These views are only useful when the underlying fields mean the same thing to everyone. Agree whether dates refer to creation, first response or a later action. Document refresh timing so the team understands when a dashboard may be behind the source system.

Check the whole journey

Consider an enquiry submitted through a website. The form creates a CRM record, assigns an owner and adds a follow-up task. The dashboard then includes that record in the appropriate view. Test what happens if a field is missing or the connection fails. Someone should be able to find and resolve the exception without silently losing the enquiry.

Keep responsibility visible

Limit access to the information each role needs, define retention practices and respect opt-out choices when planning follow-up. Review data quality alongside the dashboard rather than treating every chart as complete evidence. Connected tools can make work easier to understand, but they do not guarantee sales or other business outcomes.
