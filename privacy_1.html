Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Waviq Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") refers to Waviq Technologies Inc., a corporation registered in Ontario, Canada, and Waviq Technologies L.L.C., a limited liability company registered in Michigan, United States — operated together as one SMS and WhatsApp business automation platform serving customers in the United States and Canada. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our services or communicate with our text message (SMS) and WhatsApp bots.
Note: This policy is provided as a general template covering common US and Canadian privacy and messaging-consent requirements (including the TCPA, CTIA guidelines, applicable state privacy laws, CASL, and PIPEDA). It is not a substitute for legal advice. We recommend having a licensed attorney in the United States and Canada review this policy before it goes live, particularly given the strict penalties that can apply to non-compliant SMS marketing under US and Canadian law.

1. Who We Are

Waviq Technologies builds and operates automated SMS and WhatsApp bots for businesses across multiple industries including healthcare, fitness, restaurants, home services, and real estate, throughout the United States and Canada. Our SMS messaging programs are registered under the A2P 10DLC framework, and our WhatsApp messaging operates under Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform terms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Business Information: Business name, contact details, and registration details provided by our business clients during onboarding.
  • Messaging Account Data: Phone numbers, WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) IDs, and access credentials needed to operate your messaging program.
  • Message Data: Incoming and outgoing SMS and WhatsApp messages processed through our platform on behalf of our clients and their customers.
  • Usage Data: Message counts, conversation logs, and bot interaction data used to provide our services and generate analytics.
  • Contact Information: Names and phone numbers of end users (customers) who message our clients' SMS or WhatsApp bots.

3. SMS Messaging Program Terms (TCPA & CTIA)

Consent: By texting our number, replying to a message, or opting in through a client's sign-up form, you consent to receive SMS messages from Waviq Technologies or the business you are messaging.

Message frequency varies depending on your interactions (for example, booking confirmations, reminders, and replies to your messages).

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Not all phone numbers or carriers may be supported.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our SMS and WhatsApp automation platform
  • Process and deliver messages on behalf of our business clients
  • Manage messaging accounts and message templates for clients
  • Generate usage analytics and reports for our clients
  • Improve and develop new features for our platform
  • Comply with US A2P 10DLC / CTIA rules and Meta's WhatsApp Business policies
  • Respond to support requests and technical issues
  • Send billing and service notifications to clients

5. WhatsApp Data and the Meta Platform

Where a client also uses WhatsApp, our platform integrates with Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). By using our WhatsApp services, you acknowledge that:

  • Message data is processed through Meta's Cloud API servers
  • Meta's WhatsApp Business and Commerce Policies apply to all WhatsApp messaging
  • We access a client's WhatsApp Business Account only with their explicit authorization

6. Data Storage and Security

We take data security seriously and implement the following measures:

  • Data is stored securely on reputable cloud infrastructure
  • Access tokens and credentials are encrypted at rest
  • Client data stores are access-controlled
  • We do not sell client or end-user data to third parties for marketing purposes
  • Access to client data is restricted to authorized Waviq Technologies personnel only

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Meta Platforms: As required to provide WhatsApp messaging services
  • Telecom & messaging providers (e.g. our SMS carrier infrastructure): As required to deliver text messages
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers: For application hosting, deployment, and data storage
  • Payment processors (e.g. Stripe, Square, Interac): Where payment features are used
  • Legal authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights

8. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to provide our services:

  • Active client data is retained throughout the subscription period
  • Message logs are retained for up to 90 days for support purposes
  • After account termination, data is deleted within 30 days upon request

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable law — including US state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA for California residents) and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Where applicable, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing or messaging
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Know what categories of information we collect and why (California residents)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@waviqtech.com

10. Canada — CASL Compliance

For messages sent to recipients in Canada, we comply with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). This means:

  • We only send commercial electronic messages (including SMS and WhatsApp messages) with your express or implied consent
  • Every commercial message identifies the sender and provides a working way to unsubscribe
  • Unsubscribe requests (e.g. replying STOP) are honored within 10 business days

11. Opt-Out and Message Preferences

End users who receive messages through our clients' SMS or WhatsApp bots can opt out at any time by:

  • Replying "STOP" to any text message
  • Contacting the business directly to request removal
  • Blocking the business number

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13, in compliance with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

13. Cross-Border Data Transfers

As we use cloud services, your data may be processed in the United States, Canada, or other countries where our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify clients of significant changes via email. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
