Canada Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This policy explains how Waviq Technologies Inc. handles personal information through Waviq Canada Automation, including its SMS, WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger services.

1. Who we are

Waviq Canada Automation is operated by Waviq Technologies Inc., Canada. We provide business messaging and workflow automation services. Questions about this policy or our handling of personal information may be sent to info@waviqtech.com.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how a person interacts with a Waviq service, we may collect:

  • name, city or location, business name and business type;
  • requested service and contact information voluntarily provided by the person;
  • message content and conversation status;
  • telephone number for SMS or WhatsApp interactions;
  • a platform identifier, such as a Facebook Page-scoped user ID, and related profile information made available by the platform;
  • channel, date, time and technical records needed for security, delivery and troubleshooting; and
  • business-client configuration and support information.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, operate automated conversations, collect requested business details, arrange follow-up, provide and support contracted services, prevent misuse, maintain security, troubleshoot delivery and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Consent and choices

People initiate conversations or voluntarily provide information through the relevant messaging channel. A person may stop replying, use any channel-specific opt-out method that is offered, or contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion. Consent may be withdrawn subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice.

5. Service providers and disclosures

We may use trusted providers for messaging, hosting, artificial-intelligence processing, databases, spreadsheets, security and customer support. These providers process information only as needed to deliver their services to us. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or security, or as part of a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

6. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the person's province or outside Canada. Information processed in another jurisdiction may be subject to that jurisdiction's laws. We use reasonable contractual, organizational and technical measures appropriate to the service and information involved.

7. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, contractual requirements, dispute resolution, security and applicable law. We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Access, correction and deletion

Subject to applicable law, a person may ask to access or correct personal information or request its deletion. Instructions are available on our Data Deletion page. We may need to verify the requester's identity before acting.

9. Children

Our business automation services are not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through Waviq Canada Automation.

10. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, providers or legal obligations. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.
